Our story

Meridian began in 2006 as a small civil contractor in Mumbai. Clients kept asking us to finish what we built — so we did, adding an interiors studio and, later, a glass atelier.

Today Meridian Build Pvt. Ltd. leads every project, with Meridian Interiors and Meridian Glass Studio working inside the same programme. Fewer hand-offs, one point of accountability, and buildings that feel finished on the day they open.
